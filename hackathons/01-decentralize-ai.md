# Decentralize AI Hackathon (HackerNoon)

- Site: https://decentralizeai.tech (details below came from a pasted summary; check them against the site)
- Sponsors: HackerNoon, Nosana (GPU compute), Arweave (permanent storage), MEXC
- Round 2 deadline: **27 Feb 2027**. Half of the prize pool is paid out each round.
- How to enter: publish a technical post on HackerNoon with the right tags
- Judging: technical depth, originality, proof it works, readership and quality of the post, contribution to open AI infrastructure
- Prizes: $70 Nosana credits for each of the first 500 entrants; 15 × $450 Nosana; Arweave 1,000 AR in credits + 1,000 AR in cash; $5k in MX tokens; grand prize is the decentralizeai.tech domain and its codebase

## Assessment
- Fit: HIGH. Async, long runway, low barrier, can be done solo.
- Real contest: a working build plus a widely read post. Readership is scored.
- Winning angle: use BOTH Nosana and Arweave, so one entry competes for prizes from both sponsors.
- Action now: claim the $70 credits (first 500 only).

## Idea seeds
1. **Verifiable inference receipts**: run a model on Nosana, then write a signed receipt
   (model weights hash, container digest, prompt, output, seed) to Arweave.
   Include a public verifier page that re-runs the job and checks the result.
2. **Permanent model registry**: weights and model cards stored on Arweave,
   with one-command deploy to Nosana.
3. **Dataset provenance tool**: hashes and licenses for training data, stored permanently.
